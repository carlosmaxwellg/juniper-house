Creative guardrails

Recognizable by restraint.

A focused direction becomes credible through what it consistently refuses.

Do

  • Show natural skin and recognizable age detail.
  • Use asymmetry with disciplined alignment.
  • Make facts as visually important as atmosphere.
  • Use deep green in focused, credible moments.
  • Let materials and typography create premium character.

Don’t

  • Use spa robes, towels, candles, or cucumber imagery.
  • Lead with needles, syringes, or procedure close-ups.
  • Smooth skin into a plastic or ageless finish.
  • Fill every surface with rounded cards and shadows.
  • Use model photography as clinical proof.
